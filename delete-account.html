Account deletion

Delete your REQS. account

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open REQS. and sign in.
  2. Tap People.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all data associated with it from our servers:

  • Your profile — your name and verified phone number.
  • Your contact list — the names and phone numbers of the people you added in the app.
  • Your push notification token.
  • Every recommendation you posted.
  • Your high fives.

Data stored only on your phone (your ratings, mute and block lists, and preferences) is removed when you delete the app. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect.
